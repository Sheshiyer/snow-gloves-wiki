# Start here

You already run the business. Snow Gloves is the team that watches it with you.

You keep the decisions: what to publish, what to spend, who to hire, what never to say. The team drafts, files, routes, and waits when something is risky.

In the first hour you will:

1. Install the product.
2. Pick the agent you already talk to (Cursor, Claude, Codex, Hermes, Grok, or another).
3. Answer a short interview. Missing facts stay as `FILL:` until you give them.
4. Turn on one skill for the first job. Not a pile.

Nothing leaves the building because a model finished a paragraph. It leaves because you said yes.

## What you get

| You say | The team does | You still do |
|---|---|---|
| A job in plain language | Reads the company, drafts, cites the page it used | Keep, drop, or send |
| An event from mail or chat | Routes it to the right desk, logs it | Approve anything that spends, posts, or commits |
| A quiet week | Numbers and drafts wait until a line is crossed | Look only at what changed |
