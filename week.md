# A quiet week

A healthy room is quiet.

**Monday.** Content or numbers. A short list is waiting. You keep, drop, or develop. Nothing publishes itself.

**When a line is crossed.** Numbers speak. Growth may use the same figures. You decide what to change.

**When mail or chat arrives.** It is signed, logged, and routed. A draft or a task appears. Spend, hire, legal, and public posts wait on one yes.

**End of day.** Sentinel writes what ran, what fell back, and the top skills. You do not re-explain the business next week; corrections become rules.

**You own:** what to build, what to publish, where to spend, which opportunities are worth it.

**The team owns:** checking, research, and the draft.
